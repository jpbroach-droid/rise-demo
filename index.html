A five-minute walk-through

  1. Website → dispatch. On the website, Request service, choose "Out of service" at Capitol Square Tower, send. Open the office: it's on the dispatch board, auto-routed to Ron, with the ack timer running.
  2. Mechanic ack. In the mechanic app, tap "Got it · ETA 10 min." Back in the office the card moves to en route; in the portal the customer sees the ETA.
  3. Entrapment Clock. On Today, the live clock for SC-8841. Nothing on the screen is louder.
  4. Crossover Ledger. Gary (Hampton Roads, Marcus's territory) worked two days on the Capitol Square mod (Richmond, James's). The ledger shows what James owes Marcus at loaded cost, and the same hours sit on the job.
  5. Estimate → proposal → job. Sales → "New estimate". Cost it at loaded rates, submit, approve as Tom, send, sign it on the proposal page. The job shows up in Draw Builder with the estimate as its budget, and Performance to estimate tracks actuals against it.
  6. Mechanic utilization. Hours split maintenance / callback / repair / project per mechanic, and route margin: who has room to carry more units, whose route is being eaten by callbacks.
  7. FixFlow. Compliance → findings. Draft a quote from a Richmond citation; it lands in Sales awaiting Tom's approval, then goes out as a sign-and-pay link.
  8. Sign a proposal. Open the Bon Air modernization proposal, pick "Best," sign, submit. The pipeline card moves to Won and a job appears in Draw Builder.
  9. Scoped reports. Switch to Marcus Hale in the office. He sees Hampton Roads only.

What's named in the demo

Entrapment ClockTrapped-passenger calls get a running timer, their own escalation and automatic customer updates.
Callback Watch3+ callbacks on a unit in 90 days opens a review and a sales lead.
Crossover LedgerMechanic hours worked outside their home territory, at actual loaded cost, settled owner to owner.
FixFlowCitations, test deficiencies and mechanic findings become priced quotes with the correction deadline attached.
Inspection BookerSends the AHJ open dates, chases replies, accepts counters, needs office approval for changes.
TestTrackCat 1 / 3 / 5 due dates and the AHJ for every unit.
Certificate WatchCertificate-of-operation expirations with renewal paperwork queued.
MCP LogDigital maintenance control program per unit, written from the mechanic app.
Draw BuilderProgress billing in G702/G703 form with retainage, change orders and lien waivers.
ModRadarScores every unit for modernization and forecasts the pipeline.
StockroomParts across trucks, shops and on-order, tied to jobs and POs.
PlateScanPhotograph the data plate; make, model and capacity fill in.
Renewal RadarExpiring terms, convenience-cancel agreements and escalations not yet applied.
Field leadsA photo and a sentence from the mechanic, tracked to what sells.
Estimate builderLoaded labor rates and real part costs become the price the customer sees and the budget the job is tracked against.
Route profitabilityMaintenance revenue on each mechanic's units against their maintenance, callback and travel hours.
Proposal trackingOpens, time on page, forwards and signatures back on the proposal.
